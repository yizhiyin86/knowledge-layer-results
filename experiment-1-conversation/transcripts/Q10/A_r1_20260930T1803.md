**Where the risk is concentrated:**
- **Cyclosporine line (13,986 TRx, 84% of the at-risk volume):** all three packs come from the same cyclosporine emulsion concentrate (SEMI-031). That concentrate needs both the emulsifier premix, which has no other supplier listed at all, and Polysorbate 80. Polysorbate 80 has a second supplier on file (S-131), but S-131 is not qualified. So one supplier failure stops all three packs. Qualifying S-131 would remove one of the two exposures.
- **Oculon 2.5 mL (2,568 TRx):** the risk is packaging only. The 5 mL pack uses a different bottle with two qualified suppliers, which is why it isn't at risk.
- **Everything else is dual-qualified**, including the active ingredients (cyclosporine and latanoprost), the shared pack kit (label, carton, leaflet), caps and the buffer concentrate.

**Other finished goods:** 45 of the other 55 finished goods (the FG-30xx lens care, surgical, supplement, diagnostic and antibiotic lines) also depend on a single-source material. Their NDCs aren't in the commercial product master and have no prescription data, so I couldn't put a TRx figure on them. Their exposures include raw materials RM-10857, RM-10211, RM-11149 and RM-10360, plus Polysorbate 80 again.

**Sources and definitions**
- Fabric SUPPLY_DEMO:
  - `dbo.BOM` was broken down through every level. The hierarchy goes 9 levels deep with no circular links. Fabric doesn't support recursive queries, so I stepped through the levels one join at a time.
  - `dbo.SUPPLIER_MATERIAL`, `dbo.SUPPLIER` and `dbo.MATERIAL` for suppliers and material descriptions.
  - `dbo.MATERIAL_NDC_XREF` to link finished goods to NDCs. Its dashed NDCs were matched to the 11-digit NDCs without dashes on the Snowflake side.
- Snowflake PHARMA_DEMO: `CORE.PRODUCT` (our products are those with CO_FLG = 'Y') and `RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY` (TRX_QTY summed over the 13 most recent week-ending dates). I checked for duplicate, negative and missing rows and found none.
- **Single-source** means exactly one qualified supplier (QUALIFIED_FLG = 'Y'). Suppliers on file but not qualified don't count as a backup.
- **At-risk TRx** is the full 13-week TRx of any NDC with at least one single-source dependency. It isn't split by quantity or weighted by probability.

**Assumptions**
- I used only BOM links in effect on 2026-09-30. This left out an old link from the buffer concentrate to Polysorbate 80 that ended 2025-06-30; including it would have wrongly flagged both Oculon packs.
- I didn't use the old flat BOM table (`BOM_FLAT_LEGACY`, last updated 2024-11-30).
- Materials made in-house (bulk, filled and intermediate materials, and the pack kit) have no supplier records, so I didn't treat them as single-source. Their risk shows up through the purchased materials underneath them.
