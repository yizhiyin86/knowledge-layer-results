**What this means**
- **The biggest exposure is the dry-eye range: 13,986 TRx through a single chain.** RM-221 feeds CMP-310, which feeds the cyclosporine bulk, so Lacrivia and its approved substitute Cyclosporine OS fail together. Under rule BR-SUB-01, a disruption at S-104 or S-201 leaves no approved company substitute for Lacrivia. RM-221 has a second supplier on file (S-131), but it isn't qualified. Qualifying S-131 is the most valuable fix. CMP-310 has no second supplier at all.
- **Oculon 2.5 mL (2,568 TRx)** depends only on the S-150 dropper bottle. The 5 mL pack (986 TRx) is a partial buffer, but Oculon has no approved company substitute.
- **The other 45 exposed finished goods have no TRx data,** so I couldn't size them in TRx. They aren't in the commercial database (no NDC match in Snowflake). They are 11 lens care solutions, 10 surgical viscoelastics, 2 eye-health supplements, 11 diagnostic drops and 11 ophthalmic antibiotics. The single-source materials driving them include RM-10857 and CMP-50203 (both from S-312, tier 2, Switzerland), RM-10211 (S-344), RM-11172 and RM-11056 (both from S-504), RM-10360, RM-10288, RM-10372, CMP-50757, RM-11149, CMP-50077, RM-10054 and RM-10991. **How I got there**
- There are 24 single-source materials, each with exactly one qualified supplier (ratified metric `qualified_supplier_count` = 1, from `SUPPLY_DEMO.dbo.SUPPLIER_MATERIAL`, QUALIFIED_FLG = 'Y'). 2,093 materials have two or three. Ten of the 24 don't feed any current finished good.
- I followed each one up the bill of materials, using only current BOM entries (effective as of 30 Sep 2026) and up to 10 levels, until I reached a finished good. This follows the KL's approved "Supplier to finished goods" join path. I used `dbo.BOM` instead of the deprecated `BOM_FLAT_LEGACY`.
- `trace_supply_exposure` (S-104 / RM-221) independently confirmed the Lacrivia and Cyclosporine paths (5 BOM levels deep) and excluded an expired BOM entry (RM-221 → CMP-320, ended 30 Jun 2025).
- I matched finished goods to NDCs through `dbo.MATERIAL_NDC_XREF`, removing the dashes to join to `PHARMA_DEMO.CORE.PRODUCT`.
- TRx comes from `PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY` (the ratified demand source), summed over the 13 most recent weekly periods.
- Policy: as a supply planner you have no restrictions on these tables. I didn't use the incentive-comp goals table (`CORE.IC_GOAL`), which is restricted.

**Assumptions**
- "Single-source" means one qualified supplier. Unqualified alternates on file don't count as backup.
- "TRx at risk" is the full 13-week TRx of each exposed pack, not a modelled loss. It ignores inventory on hand and recovery lead times, which `get_lead_times` can add if you need them.
- Single-sourced in-house manufacturing steps, such as a plant that is the only one making a bulk, are out of scope. This analysis covers purchased materials only.
