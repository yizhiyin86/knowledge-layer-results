## Final answer

**Lacrivia had the highest claim rejection rate of any company product: 18.5% over the last 13 weeks** (2,367 rejected out of 12,809 adjudicated claims). Its 0.1% 10 mL pack was the worst single pack at **20.6%**, and the 0.1% 5 mL pack was at **17.4%**. **Prior authorization is the main cause.**

**Company products, last 13 weeks (rejected ÷ paid + rejected)**

| Product | Rejected / adjudicated | Rejection rate | Main reject group |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lacrivia 0.1% 10 mL** | 886 / 4,311 | **20.6%** | Prior authorization |
| **Lacrivia 0.1% 5 mL** | 1,481 / 8,498 | **17.4%** | Prior authorization |
| Oculon 0.005% 5 mL | 174 / 1,160 | 15.0% | Step therapy |

**What's driving Lacrivia's rejections**
1. **Prior authorization: 58% of rejections** (1,372 claims, about 10.7 points of the 18.5% rate). The three prior-authorization reject codes are close in size:
   - "PA Exhausted/Not Renewable": 500
   - "Prior Authorization Required": 439
   - "Prior Authorization Denied": 433

   Expired authorizations not being renewed are the largest piece, which points to continuing patients failing re-authorization as well as new starts.
2. **Not covered: 22% of rejections** (513 claims, about 4 points of the rate). These are "Product Not On Formulary" (280) and "Product/Service Not Covered" (233).
3. **Other groups are small:** refill/DUR 160, plan limits 148, other 104, distribution limits 52.

**Where it's concentrated**
- **Northstar Health Commercial (pharmacy benefit manager: OptiScript)** has the biggest prior-authorization problem: 35% rejection rate, 32% from prior authorization alone. Its 225 prior-authorization rejections are 16% of all Lacrivia prior-authorization rejections.
- **Formulary gaps** cause very high rejection rates at a few plans, mostly from "not covered" rejections:

| Plan | Rejection rate | "Not covered" rejections |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen Health Plans Medicare Part D | 60% | 100 |
| Granite Plains Managed Medicaid | 54% | 36 |
| Harborview Health Exchange | 50% | 88 |
| Harborview Commercial East | 45% | 88 |
| Redwood Commercial East | 32% | 27 |

- **This is a long-running problem, not a new spike.** Lacrivia's monthly rejection rate has stayed between 17% and 22% since October 2025. The 10 mL pack's prior-authorization rate is always higher (11–14%) than the 5 mL pack's (9–10%).

**What happens to patients after a rejection**
- **25% abandon the prescription** (601 of 2,367).
- **37% are switched to a competitor** (878): Tearsol 428, generic cyclosporine 204, Cyclomist 149, Hydrova 97.
- **No rejected Lacrivia claim was switched to the company's own Cyclosporine OS.**

**Possible next steps:** support with prior-authorization renewals (especially at Northstar/OptiScript), formulary contracting with Evergreen Part D and Harborview, and a look at why patients aren't being moved to Cyclosporine OS.
