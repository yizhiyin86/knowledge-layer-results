## Final answer

**LACRIVIA (cyclosporine 0.1%) had the highest claim rejection rate of our three products in the last 13 weeks: 18.5%, or 2,367 rejected out of 12,809 adjudicated claims.** The main cause is prior authorization, which accounts for 58% of its rejections. Formulary exclusions at a few specific plans are the second cause.

| Product | Paid | Rejected | Rejection rate | Previous 13 weeks |

For comparison, the main competing dry-eye brand, TEARSOL, is at 18.2%, so LACRIVIA's rate is in line with other branded products in the category.

**What drives LACRIVIA's rejections**
1. **Prior authorization: 58% of rejections, 10.7% of all claims, flat versus the previous 13 weeks.** It splits roughly evenly into three reasons:
   - The existing authorization ran out or can't be renewed (code PA): 500 claims.
   - An authorization was required and not on file (code 75): 439 claims.
   - The authorization was denied (code 3N): 433 claims.
   
   New and continuing patients are rejected at about the same rate (about 17–21% each). Since one in three of these rejections is an expired authorization, renewals are a problem too, not just getting new patients started.
   - **Northstar Health Commercial (OptiScript)** is the biggest single contributor: 249 rejections at a 35% rate, 225 of them for prior authorization.
   - Prior authorization rejections are also high (about 17–18% of claims) across Northstar, Keystone and Lakeshore plans.
2. **Not covered or not on formulary: 22% of rejections, 4.0% of claims, up from 3.5% in the previous 13 weeks.** This is the only cause that is growing, and it is concentrated in a few plans:
   - Evergreen Medicare Part D (ClearPath Rx): 60% rejection rate, 100 claims not covered.
   - Harborview Health Exchange and Commercial East (Vantage PBM): 51% and 45% rejection rates, 88 not covered at each.
   - Granite Plains Commercial and Managed Medicaid (OptiScript), and Harborview Managed Medicaid.
3. **Everything else is small:** days-supply and plan limits 1.2% of claims, refill-too-soon and drug-interaction checks 1.3%, specialty or limited-distribution pharmacy required 0.4%, other 1.0%. Step therapy doesn't apply to LACRIVIA; it only affects OCULON, at 4.3% of claims.

By payer type, the highest rates are Health Exchange (27.6%, mostly not-covered) and Managed Medicaid (23.6%). The biggest volume of rejections is in Commercial (1,216 claims, 17.7%). Cash and discount-card claims are never rejected. Both pack sizes are affected; the 10 mL pack rejects slightly more often (20.5% vs 17.4% for 5 mL).

**Suggested focus:** simplify prior authorization and renewals with Northstar/OptiScript, and look at formulary position at Evergreen Part D, Harborview and Granite Plains.

**Sources, definitions and assumptions**
- Claims come from PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ. Products were matched on NDC to CORE.PRODUCT, where the company flag marks LACRIVIA, OCULON and CYCLOSPORINE OS as ours.
