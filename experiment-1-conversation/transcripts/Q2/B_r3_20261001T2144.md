## Final answer

**Lacrivia had the highest claim rejection rate of any company product: 18.4% over the last 13 weeks.** That's 2,367 rejected out of 12,838 paid or rejected claims. Prior-authorization rejections drive most of it (58%). The next biggest driver is the product not being covered, which is concentrated in a few plans that leave it off their formulary.

**Company products, last 13 weeks** (rejected ÷ (paid + rejected))
| Product | Rejected | Paid | Rejection rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lacrivia** | 2,367 | 10,471 | **18.4%** |
| Oculon | 594 | 3,560 | 14.3% |
| Cyclosporine OS 0.05% | 223 | 3,545 | 5.9% |

Lacrivia's rate is about the same as its main competitor Tearsol (18.1%). Only Pressura (19.0%), a competitor product, is higher across all products.

**What's driving Lacrivia's rejections**
1. **Prior authorization: 1,372 rejections, 58% of the total, 10.7 points of the 18.4% rate.**
   - The three codes are almost evenly split: PA expired or not renewable (500), PA required (439) and PA denied (433).
   - Expired PAs are the single largest reject code. That points to a renewal or re-authorization problem for existing patients, not just new starts.
   - PA rejections are spread across many plans. The biggest source is Northstar Health Commercial: 25.7% rejection rate, 21.5% from PA alone, 275 rejections (12% of all Lacrivia rejections). Next are Bluecrest Commercial, Redwood Commercial (15.9% PA rate), Keystone Medicare Part D, Lakeshore Managed Medicaid and Lakeshore Part D, and Silverline Part D (about 18–20% PA rates).
2. **Not covered: 513 rejections, 22%, 4.0 points of the rate.**
   - Codes are "not on formulary" (280) and "not covered" (233).
   - About 85% come from about 8 plans that exclude the product:
     - Evergreen Health Plans Part D: 59.6% rejection rate, 43% not covered
     - Harborview Health Exchange: 50.2% rejection rate, 31% not covered
     - Harborview Health Commercial: 10.8% not covered
     - Harborview Health Managed Medicaid: 19.9% not covered
     - Granite Plains Managed Medicaid: 54.4% rejection rate, 32% not covered
     - Granite Plains Commercial: 14.2% not covered
     - Bluecrest Exchange: 33% not covered
     - Tidewater Managed Medicaid: 19.8% not covered
3. **Smaller drivers (about 20% combined):** refill too soon or drug-use review rejects (7%), days-supply and plan limits (6%), other reasons such as coordination of benefits or patient not covered (5%), and specialty or limited-distribution pharmacy required (2%). There were no step-therapy rejections.

**Other context**
- **Stable, not a new problem:** Lacrivia's monthly rejection rate has stayed between about 17.6% and 18.9% for the past year, and the PA share has held at about 10–11 points.
- **By pack:** the 10 mL pack is rejected more often (20.5%) than the 5 mL (17.4%). New and continuing patients are rejected at similar rates.
- **What happens after a rejection:** 25.4% of rejected Lacrivia claims were abandoned. 37% (878) were switched to another product, mostly the competitor Tearsol (428), then generic cyclosporine (204), Cyclomist (149) and Hydrova (97). None were switched to the company's own Cyclosporine OS.

**Suggested focus for Market Access:** first, PA renewal and support at Northstar Commercial and the Part D and Managed Medicaid plans with high PA rates. Second, formulary access at Evergreen Part D, Harborview (Exchange, Commercial and Managed Medicaid) and Granite Plains.
