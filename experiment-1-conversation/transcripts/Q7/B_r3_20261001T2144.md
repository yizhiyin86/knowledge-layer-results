**How long until supply recovers (from the knowledge layer's standard production lead times)**

- Some suppliers feeding Lacrivia shipped late recently: 10 mL dropper bottles (supplier S-105) 20 days late, received 9/22; castor oil (S-103) 18 days late, shipped 9/30; polysorbate 80 (S-104, which has no qualified backup) repeatedly 14–20 days late.
