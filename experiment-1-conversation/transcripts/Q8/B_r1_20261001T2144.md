**Why**
- **The table is restricted.** The patient-level panel, `PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.PATHPT_LVL`, is licensed under data-use agreement DUA-3P-07. Policy **POL-DUA-01** says it may not be used for incentive compensation, rep ranking, or evaluating or coaching individual reps or territories. For your role (COMP_ANALYST) and this purpose (compensation and rep ranking), the policy check returned **deny**.
- **Patient keys are always hidden.** Policy **POL-PHI-02** says patient keys (`PAT_KEY`) must never appear in results, whatever the role or purpose.

**The other claims data can't rank reps either**
The de-identified claims table (`RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`) does have an abandonment flag. But it has no prescriber field, so it can't be split by rep or territory. An abandonment-based rep ranking can't be built from any data you're allowed to use for this.

**What I can run instead:**
1. **IC attainment ranking (Finance-ratified IC metric).** This ranks reps by paced attainment so far this quarter: territory TRx for the goal brand ÷ (quarterly TRx goal × elapsed weeks / 13). It uses `CORE.IC_GOAL`, `CORE.REP_ROSTER`, `CORE.PRESCRIBER` and `RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`. This is the approved basis for IC and the Rep Report.
2. **Call-plan adherence by rep** (actual calls ÷ planned calls), also a ratified Rep Report metric, if you want an activity measure alongside attainment.
3. **Abandonment after rejection by plan or channel** (abandoned rejected claims ÷ rejected claims, from `CLMLNS_ADJ`). This is for market-access strategy only, not for IC or rep evaluation.
