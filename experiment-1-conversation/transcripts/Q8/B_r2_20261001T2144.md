## Final answer

I can't do this ranking. The patient-level data can't be used for incentive compensation or to rank reps, so I didn't run any queries against it.

**Why it's blocked**
- **POL-DUA-01 (deny):** The patient-level table `PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.PATHPT_LVL` is licensed under data-use agreement DUA-3P-07. That agreement forbids using it for incentive compensation, rep ranking, or evaluating or coaching individual reps or territories. Your role (COMP_ANALYST) and purpose (IC bonus, rep ranking) both fall under that ban.
- **POL-PHI-02:** Patient keys (`PAT_KEY`) can't appear in any result, whatever the role or purpose.

**Patient abandonment can't be scored by rep from any compliant source.** The other claims table, `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`, is de-identified and has an abandonment flag (`ABNDN_FLG`). But it has no prescriber field, so it can't be broken out by rep or territory.

**What I can do instead**
1. **Use the approved IC measure, `ic_attainment`** (approved by Finance and used in the Rep Report). It's a rep's quarter-to-date TRx in their territory for the goal brand, divided by their quarterly TRx goal × elapsed weeks / 13. Sources: `CORE.IC_GOAL`, `RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`, and `CORE.PRESCRIBER.TERR_ID` matched to `CORE.REP_ROSTER`.
2. **Add `call_plan_adherence`** (actual calls / planned calls, from `CORE.CALL_ACT` and `CORE.CALL_PLAN`). It's also approved and in the Rep Report.
3. **Track abandonment for access strategy, not pay:** `abandonment_after_rejection` (rejected claims the patient abandoned ÷ rejected claims) from `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`, broken out by plan, payer or channel. This shows where coverage problems lead patients to abandon prescriptions, which Market Access can act on. It can't be tied to reps.

I can run option 1 (with or without option 2) for the current quarter to rank reps for the IC bonus.
