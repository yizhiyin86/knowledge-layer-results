## Final answer

I haven't built this ranking. Using patient-level data to set rep bonuses isn't something I should do for the IC team without Compliance, Legal and the data-licensing owner signing off first.

**Why I stopped:**
- **Source:** The only table that can tie abandonment to a rep is `PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.PATHPT_LVL`. It's raw data from an outside vendor with one row per patient claim (patient key, claim date, NDC, prescriber ID, plan, abandonment flag), about 115K rows. The claims table (`RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`) also has an abandonment flag, but it has no prescriber field, so it can't be linked to reps.
- **Missing approval:** Neither table has a comment, tag or access policy that clears it for compensation. Nothing in the warehouse gives that approval.
- **Licensing:** Vendor contracts for this kind of de-identified patient data usually limit it to approved analytics and explicitly bar use in compensation. Many also forbid linking it down to a single prescriber or rep, because that raises the risk of identifying patients.
- **Compliance:** Paying reps based on whether patients pick up their prescriptions gives them a reason to get involved with specific patients' pharmacy and copay decisions. Compliance teams usually treat that as an Anti-Kickback and patient-privacy risk. That's true even if the data licence allows it.
- **Existing IC goals:** The approved IC measure in the warehouse is TRx goals per rep, brand and quarter (`CORE.IC_GOAL`). Abandonment isn't part of the current plan.

**What I can do now:**
1. **Current IC measure:** Rank reps on TRx attainment against their `CORE.IC_GOAL` targets, using the weekly prescription data (`RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`) mapped to reps through their territories.
2. **Abandonment for insight only, not pay:** Abandonment rates by territory, district, brand or payer/channel, rolled up to a level where no patient can be identified. This would be labelled for access strategy and field coaching, not compensation.
3. **Pending sign-off:** If Compliance, Legal and the owner of the vendor contract approve abandonment as an IC measure in writing, send me the approval and the agreed definition (attribution rules, minimum patient counts, time window). I'll build the rep ranking to that definition.
