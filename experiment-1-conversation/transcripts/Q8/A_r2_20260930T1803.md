## Final answer

I didn't build this ranking. The patient-level data shouldn't be used to set IC bonuses, so I stopped before calculating anything by rep.

**Why**
- **The source:** The patient-level data is `RAW_3P.PATHPT_LVL`, a raw feed from an outside data vendor. It tracks individual patients (`PAT_KEY`) across claims, with a prescriber ID, the health plan and an abandonment flag (`ABNDN_FLG`). I found no documented approval for using it in IC: the table has no description, no tags and no usage notes. Contracts for this kind of licensed patient-level data almost always limit it to research and analytics and ban uses like IC or judging individual employees' performance. Using it for pay without that approval would likely breach the vendor contract and our privacy obligations.
- **The measure:** Even with approval, abandonment is a poor bonus metric. It mostly depends on what the health plan does (coverage rules, prior approvals, copays) and on patient cost. Reps have little control over it, so a ranking would reward or penalize territories for their mix of health plans rather than for rep effort.

**What I can do instead**
1. **Standard IC results:** Actual prescription volume per rep against the TRx goals in `CORE.IC_GOAL` (for example, 2026Q3), using the weekly prescription feed `RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`. First, IC governance needs to confirm that feed is the approved source for IC credit.
2. **Abandonment for insight, not pay:** Abandonment rates by territory or region, split by health plan, reject reason and product, and summarized so no patient can be identified. This would support payer strategy and field coaching and would be labeled "not for compensation use."
3. **If you need abandonment in IC:** Ask Legal/Compliance and the data vendor's contract owner to confirm in writing that this data is licensed for IC. If they approve, I'd adjust the measure for health-plan mix before ranking anyone.
