## Final answer

**Short answer:** Only one table can feed "paid rate": the claims table `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ` in Snowflake. I can't tell you whether the Payer Report and the Rep Report define it the same way. Neither report's logic, nor any written definition of "paid rate", is stored in Snowflake or Fabric. The data does show two things, though. The same claims give anywhere from **85.1% to 90.0%** depending on the rules used. And a rep-level paid rate can't be built straight from claims, so the Rep Report must be using a different method. You should assume the two numbers aren't comparable until the report owners confirm their logic.

**What feeds paid rate**
| Role | Table | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paid and rejected status | `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ` | 287,395 claim lines, one per claim ID, dated 27 Sep 2025 – 30 Sep 2026. Status codes: P = paid (244,448), R = rejected (36,258), V = reversed (6,689). Also carries reject reason, secondary-payer, abandoned, substitution and patient-type flags. |
| Reject reasons | `REF.REJ_CODE` | 16 codes in 7 groups (not covered, prior authorisation, step therapy, refill/DUR, plan limits, distribution limits, other). Every reject code in the claims matches this table. |
| Payer and channel rollup | `CORE.PAYER_SPINE` + `REF.CHNL` | Plan → payer → PBM → channel. All claim plans match. |
| Backup copy | `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ_BKP_2025` | 77,757 lines. It is an exact copy of the Q4-2025 claims (same IDs, same statuses), so no restatement. Using it alongside the main table would double-count. |
| Not a source | Fabric `SUPPLY_DEMO` | Supply chain only. No claim, plan or payer columns. |

**Why the definitions are likely to differ**
1. **Rules change the answer by about 5 points** (full year of claims):
   - Paid ÷ all lines, including reversals: **85.06%**
   - Paid ÷ (paid + rejected), reversals left out: **87.08%**
   - Same, primary payer only: **87.08%**
   - Same, cash/discount channel also left out: **86.44%**. Cash claims are always 100% paid, which inflates a blended rate.
   - Primary payer only, refill-too-soon and DUR rejects left out: **88.22%**
   - Primary payer only, abandoned claims left out: **89.95%**
2. **Level of detail:** claims have a plan but no prescriber. The Payer Report can roll claims up by plan, payer, PBM and channel directly. The Rep Report can't tie claims to prescribers, territories or reps. It must either estimate a rep's rate from their plan mix in the weekly prescription data (`RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`, which has prescriber and plan but no paid/rejected status) or use some other source. Either way, a rep's "paid rate" is an estimate, not a count of adjudicated claims.
3. **Channel mix:** by channel (paid ÷ (paid + rejected)), Commercial is 86.4%, Part D 86.0%, Exchange 84.0%, Medicaid fee-for-service 89.5%, Managed Medicaid 88.3% and Cash 100%. A report that includes or weights channels differently will show a different number.
