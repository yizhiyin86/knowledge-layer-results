## Final answer

**Answer:** Both reports use the same source, but they define paid rate differently, so their numbers don't match.

- **Source:** Paid rate comes from a single field, `ADJ_STAT` in `PHARMA_DEMO.RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ` (pharmacy claim lines; P = paid, R = rejected, V = reversed). Channel and payer cuts are added by joining `CORE.PAYER_SPINE` and `REF.CHNL`. Neither report should use the deprecated `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ_BKP_2025`.
- **Payer Report:** Uses the ratified `paid_rate`, which is paid ÷ (paid + rejected). Reversed claims are left out of both the top and bottom of the ratio. Market Access ratified this definition.
- **Rep Report:** Uses `paid_rate_rep_v1`, an older version that is not ratified. It is paid ÷ all claim lines, so reversed claims count as "not paid" and pull the rate down.

**How big the gap is** (all claims, 2025‑09‑27 to 2026‑09‑30, 287,395 lines: 244,448 paid, 36,258 rejected, 6,689 reversed):

| Scope | Payer Report (ratified) | Rep Report (v1) | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **87.1%** | **85.1%** | **−2.0 pts** |
| Commercial | 86.4% | 84.4% | −2.0 |
| Medicare Part D | 86.0% | 84.0% | −2.0 |
| Managed Medicaid | 88.3% | 86.2% | −2.1 |
| Health Exchange | 84.0% | 82.1% | −1.9 |
| Medicaid FFS | 89.5% | 87.3% | −2.2 |
| Cash / Discount | 100.0% | 97.0% | −3.0 |

The Rep Report always comes out lower, by about the share of claims that were reversed (2.3–3.1% depending on channel). The gap is widest for Cash / Discount. That channel has no rejections, so the ratified rate is 100% by definition, and reversals account for the whole gap.

**Recommendation:** Use the ratified `paid_rate` (paid ÷ (paid + rejected)) for any Market Access work. The Rep Report should either switch to it or be footnoted as a non-ratified variant. If you need to report reversals, track them as a separate rate instead of folding them into paid rate.

**Sources and definitions used:**
- Metric definitions `paid_rate` (ratified) and `paid_rate_rep_v1` (not ratified), both computed from `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ.ADJ_STAT`.
- Ratified tables: `RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`, `CORE.PAYER_SPINE`, `REF.CHNL`.
- Deprecated-table notice: `CLMLNS_ADJ_BKP_2025` is replaced by `CLMLNS_ADJ`.
- Policy: none of these tables is restricted for the MARKET_ACCESS role. The only restricted table, `CORE.IC_GOAL`, wasn't used.

**Assumptions:** Figures cover every claim in the data (the full year up to 2026‑09‑30), not just the last 13 weeks. No `ADJ_STAT` values other than P, R or V were found.
