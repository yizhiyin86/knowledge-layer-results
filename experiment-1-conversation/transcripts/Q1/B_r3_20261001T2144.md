**What's behind the trend**
- **Commercial (about half the market) is the growth engine.** Lacrivia's Commercial TRx grew 23% between the first and last 4 weeks while the market grew only 1%, so this is real share gain. Share stepped up in late July (to about 25%) and again in mid-August (to about 27–29%). Over the same time, Lacrivia's paid rate in Commercial edged up from 81.3% to 83.0%.
- **Part D is losing ground.** Part D market TRx grew 9.6%, but Lacrivia's Part D TRx fell 10%, and weekly share has been about 17–19% since the week of 2026-08-14. Lacrivia's Part D paid rate held steady (79.7% to 80.5%), so claim rejections don't explain the drop. Competitors appear to be taking the new Part D volume. A next step would be to look at Part D formulary positions and which competitors are gaining.
- **The smaller channels are noisy.** Medicaid FFS, Cash and Exchange each have only about 145–230 market TRx a week, so single weeks swing ±5 pts. Their changes are directional at best. Managed Medicaid is worth watching: share is sliding slightly and paid rate fell from 78.0% to 75.0%.
