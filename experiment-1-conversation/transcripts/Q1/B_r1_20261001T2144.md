## Final answer

Over the last 13 weeks (weeks ending 3 Jul to 25 Sep 2026), Lacrivia held **23.7% of TRx in the dry-eye market** (10,442 of 43,975 TRx). That share went up from 22.8% in the first 4 weeks to 24.6% in the last 4. Almost all of the gain came from Commercial. Medicare Part D is the one channel clearly losing share.

**How each channel is trending:**
- **Commercial (about half of market volume):** share rose almost every week, from 23.1% (3 Jul) to 29.3% (25 Sep). There was a clear step up in mid-August, to about 27% from the 14 Aug week, and weekly TRx went from about 390 to about 490. This is the main reason total share is growing.
- **Medicare Part D:** share drifted from about 21–22% in July (with a one-off 26.3% in the 17 Jul week) to 17–19% in August and September. Lacrivia's weekly TRx stayed roughly flat (about 155–170), so the market grew around it rather than Lacrivia losing scripts. That points to competitors gaining in Part D, which is worth checking against formulary positions and paid rates.
- **Smaller channels** (Medicaid FFS, Managed Medicaid, Health Exchange, Cash) have only 30–90 Lacrivia TRx a week, so weekly share moves 5–10 points. Read their trends as directional only. Cash spiked to about 31% in late August and early September, then fell back to about 21%.
