**What's behind the trend**
- **Commercial:** Weekly Lacrivia TRx rose from 388 to 489 while the market stayed flat at about 1,600–1,700 a week. Share went from 23.1% to 29.3%, and the jump started in mid-August. That looks like a real pull-through gain, possibly from an access or formulary change. It's worth checking against formulary data.
- **Medicare Part D:** Lacrivia TRx stayed flat at about 150–180 a week while the market grew from 823 to 920 a week. Lacrivia isn't capturing the new Part D volume, so share fell from about 22% to about 18%. This is the channel to look into (coverage tier, prior-authorization or step-edit rejections).
- **Health Exchange, Medicaid FFS and Cash:** These channels only have about 140–230 market TRx a week, so weekly share jumps around a lot. Treat their trends as directional only.

**Sources and definitions**
- **Source:** `PHARMA_DEMO.MART.PYR_SHR_WK`. Share is Lacrivia TRx divided by total dry-eye market TRx within each channel. I checked every week and channel of the last 13 weeks against the raw weekly Rx data (`RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`) and it matched exactly.
- **Market (dry eye):** Defined by the market-definition file `BIZ.MKT_DEF_DRIVER` (v7). It includes Lacrivia (both pack sizes), Cyclosporine OS, Cyclomist, Tearsol, Hydrova and generic cyclosporine.
- **Channels:** Decoded with `REF.CHNL`. The channel codes in the Rx data match the plan-to-channel mapping in `CORE.PAYER_SPINE` for every record.
- **Last 13 weeks:** The weeks ending 3 Jul to 25 Sep 2026, which are the latest weeks in the data.
- **Table I didn't use:** I left out `MART.PYR_SHR_WK_OLD`. It divides by all eye-care TRx, including glaucoma products, so it understates share (for example, 17.95% vs. the correct 23.61% for Commercial in one week).
- **Assumptions:** Totals are weighted by TRx volume, not simple averages of weekly shares. Cash / Discount isn't a payer, but I kept it in because it's one of the defined channels. The trend slope is a simple straight-line fit over the 13 weekly shares.
- **Data use:** I only used TRx totals by channel. No patient-level or prescriber-level data went into the results, which is appropriate for a market access audience.
