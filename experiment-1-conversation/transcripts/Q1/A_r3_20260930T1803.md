**How it's trending**
- **Commercial is driving the growth.** Weekly share rose from about 23% in early July to about 29% by late September (29.28% in the latest week). The gain has held since mid-August. It came mostly from generic cyclosporine (down 2.0 points) and Tearsol (down 1.8 points). Commercial is about half of market volume, so this one channel lifts the total.
- **Medicare Part D is the concern.** Share fell from about 22% to about 18% (17–19% every week since mid-August), and the drop started around mid-July. Cyclomist (+1.3), generic cyclosporine (+1.2) and Tearsol (+1.1) all took share. A steady, Part D-only drop like this often points to a coverage or formulary change for 2026. It's worth checking Part D formulary status and claim rejects.
- **Smaller channels** (Managed Medicaid, Health Exchange, Medicaid FFS, Cash) have only about 150–340 market TRx a week. Their weekly share swings 5–10 points, so treat their direction as a signal, not a firm trend. Managed Medicaid is essentially flat.
