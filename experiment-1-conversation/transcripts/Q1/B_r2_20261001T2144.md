**How it's trending**
- **Commercial (about half of market TRx) is the growth engine.** Weekly share rose almost every week, from 23.1% to 29.3%. Lacrivia TRx grew from about 390 to about 489 a week while the market stayed flat at about 1,650–1,690 a week. The gain came mainly from Tearsol (25.1% → 23.3%) and generic cyclosporine (20.1% → 18.0%).
- **Medicare Part D is the watch-out.** Share went from 21.9% to 18.3%. The 26.4% in the week of 17 Jul was a one-week spike. Lacrivia volume stayed flat at about 155–180 TRx a week while the Part D market grew from about 820 to 920 a week. Competitors took that growth: Cyclomist rose from 14.1% to 15.3%, generic cyclosporine from 20.0% to 21.2% and Tearsol from 24.5% to 25.7%. That points to a coverage or access question in Part D. A useful next step is to check Part D paid rate and reject reasons in the claims data.
- **Medicaid FFS, Managed Medicaid, Health Exchange and Cash are small channels.** Each has about 140–350 market TRx a week, so weekly share swings of ±5 points are mostly noise. Their 13-week shares sit at about 22–24% with no clear direction. The Cash rise peaked at 31.7% in early September and has since fallen back to about 21%.
