- **Dry-eye prescriber:** a high-decile prescriber with at least one dry-eye prescription in the last 13 weekly periods (weeks ending up to 2026-09-25). Weekly prescription counts come from `RAW_3P.RXT_WKLY`. Dry-eye products (LACRIVIA, CYCLOSPORINE OS, TEARSOL, CYCLOMIST, HYDROVA and generic cyclosporine) come from the market-definition file `BIZ.MKT_DEF_DRIVER`.
- **Calls:** `CORE.CALL_ACT` joined to `CORE.REP_ROSTER` to get each rep's team. I counted all call types (in-person detail, lunch, sample drop and virtual) from every rep. INDIGO reps cover whole districts rather than territories, so I matched calls by prescriber, not by territory; matching by territory would miss their calls.
- **Call plan:** 2026 Q3 targets from `CORE.CALL_PLAN`.

**Assumptions and caveats**
- **Decile isn't dry-eye only:** four decile 8–10 prescribers don't write dry-eye at all. Three write only glaucoma and one has no prescription data. I left them out; all four had a call in the window anyway.
- **Window:** "last 90 days" means 2 Jul – 30 Sep 2026, counting the 30 Sep data date. With a 91-day window, Jordan Stroud and Taylor Mercer would drop off the list.
- **Excluded data:** I didn't use the scratch tables (such as the old 2024 decile table or the draft Q2 territory alignment) or any patient-level data. The output only has prescriber-level information suitable for field use.
