| Material | S-104's role | Other suppliers on file | Qualified backup? |
|---|---|---|---|
| RM-221 Polysorbate 80 NF, low-peroxide | Qualified, primary | S-131 Pinecrest Chemical Trading (China): **not qualified** | **No. S-104 is the only qualified source** |
| RM-222 Benzalkonium chloride 50% | Qualified, primary | S-130 Oakridge Chemicals (US): **qualified**, backup | **Yes** |

Order history supports this. S-130 is already an active RM-222 supplier: 12 current orders for 49,400 units, plus 3 archived orders for 5,500. S-104 still carries most of the volume: about 160k units of RM-222 and 167k of RM-221 on current orders. S-131 has never had an order for RM-221.

**Products still exposed: 14, all through RM-221**

I traced RM-221 up the current bill of materials, counting only links in effect on 2026-09-30:
- **Cyclosporine eye emulsions** (RM-221 → emulsifier premix CMP-310 → emulsion concentrate SEMI-031 → fill):
  - FG-1001 Lacrivia 0.1% ophthalmic emulsion 5 mL (NDC 99101-0101-05)
  - FG-1002 Lacrivia 0.1% ophthalmic emulsion 10 mL (NDC 99101-0101-10)
  - FG-1003 Cyclosporine 0.05% ophthalmic emulsion 5 mL (NDC 99101-0202-05)
- **Lens care solutions 1–11** (FG-3001 to FG-3011, NDCs 99101-4001 to 4011): RM-221 feeds two intermediates, INT-LC1011 and INT-LC1035, which pass through several more intermediate levels, seven steps up the bill of materials in total.

**Covered, but capacity needs checking**
- FG-2001 and FG-2002, Oculon (latanoprost) 0.005% ophthalmic solution 2.5 mL and 5 mL, use RM-222 through CMP-320 and SEMI-060. S-130 covers them on paper, but it now takes a small share of volume. It would need to handle roughly 3× its current RM-222 volume (about 210k units over the past year versus 49k).
- CMP-320 used to contain RM-221 too, but that link ended on 2025-06-30, so Oculon has no exposure to RM-221.

**Recommended actions**
1. Speed up qualifying a second RM-221 source, either by finishing S-131's qualification or finding another supplier.
2. Until then, build safety stock of RM-221.
3. Confirm S-130 can take on the full RM-222 volume.
