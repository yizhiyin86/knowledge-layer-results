## Final answer

**Answer: No.** S-104 (Delmar Specialty Chemicals, a tier-2 supplier in India) provides two materials, and only one of them has a qualified alternate supplier. If S-104 went down, 14 finished goods would still have no qualified supply: Lacrivia 5 mL and 10 mL, Cyclosporine 0.05% 5 mL, and 11 lens-care solutions. Oculon would be covered.

**By material**

| Material | S-104 role | Other suppliers | Qualified alternate? | Finished goods affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RM-221 Polysorbate 80 NF, low-peroxide | Primary, qualified | S-131 Pinecrest Chemical Trading (China): **not qualified** | **No (single source)** | 14 products, listed below |
| RM-222 Benzalkonium chloride 50% | Primary, qualified | S-130 Oakridge Chemicals (US): qualified, secondary | **Yes** | Oculon 2.5 mL and 5 mL (covered) |

S-130 already supplies RM-222: it has 11 closed orders (45,700 units) and 1 open order (3,700 units), compared with 48 closed orders (157,600 units) from S-104. So moving supply to S-130 is mainly a question of how much more it can produce; no new qualification is needed.

**Products that would still have no qualified supply (all through RM-221, using current recipe links only)**
- **FG-1001 Lacrivia 0.1% 5 mL** (NDC 99101-0101-05). Path: RM-221 → CMP-310 → SEMI-031 → SEMI-044 → FILL-1001, 5 levels down.
- **FG-1002 Lacrivia 0.1% 10 mL** (NDC 99101-0101-10), 5 levels down.
- **FG-1003 Cyclosporine 0.05% 5 mL** (NDC 99101-0202-05), 5 levels down, through the same component chain (CMP-310 → SEMI-031).
- **FG-3001 to FG-3011, lens-care solutions 1–11** (NDCs 99101-4001-91 to 99101-4011-15), 7 levels down through INT-LC1011 and INT-LC2002. These aren't in the commercial database, so there is no prescription data to size their demand.

**Other risks to flag**
- **No company substitute for Lacrivia.** The approved substitute rule (BR-SUB-01) names Cyclosporine OS 0.05% as Lacrivia's substitute, but that product depends on the same RM-221 supply, so it would be short too. Medical Affairs would need to guide therapeutic alternatives.
- **CMP-310 is also single-sourced.** This emulsifier premix comes only from S-201 Larchmont Contract Manufacturing, a tier-1 supplier. Because S-104 ships RM-221 to S-201, an S-104 outage would stop CMP-310 as well.
- **Oculon is not exposed through RM-221.** RM-221 used to feed Oculon through CMP-320, but that recipe link ended on 2025-06-30, so I left it out.
- **S-104 has open orders.** There are 3 still open: 2 for RM-221 (7,500 units) and 1 for RM-222 (2,600 units), the latest placed 2026-10-02.

**Suggested next steps:** speed up qualifying S-131 for RM-221 (or find another polysorbate 80 source), and confirm how much more RM-222 S-130 can supply. Before deciding on allocation, run lead-time and days-of-supply checks for FG-1001, FG-1002 and FG-1003. Recovery takes longer for eye products because each batch needs a 14-day sterility test before release (BR-LEAD-01).
