**What this means for coaching**
- **No territory is meaningfully different from the 6.4% average.** Most territories have 8–28 abandoned prescriptions, so a rate can easily move ±3 points by chance. You can see this in the swings between periods (T014 went from 2.3% to 6.8%; T054 from 4.0% to 6.8%). Don't rank reps on this or use it in performance reviews.
- **T012 is the only territory above 8% in both periods** (8.4%, then 8.2%). It's the most reasonable place to look more closely, but even there the gap from average isn't conclusive.
- **Coach on access, not persuasion.** Across all Lacrivia abandoned claims in the same 13 weeks (850 claims, a different dataset that can't be split by territory):
  - 44% were rejected for prior authorization.
  - 29% were reversed at the pharmacy counter.
  - 15% were rejected because the drug isn't covered.
  
  So coaching should focus on helping offices with prior authorization and coverage issues, since that's where most abandonment comes from.

**Sources and definitions**
- **Territory figures:** third-party patient-level prescription data (`RAW_3P.PATHPT_LVL`). A prescription counts as abandoned when its abandonment flag is `Y`. The rate is abandoned prescriptions divided by all prescriptions.
- **Lacrivia:** both pack sizes (NDCs 99101010105 and 99101010110), from `CORE.PRODUCT`.
- **Territory assignment:** each prescriber's current territory in `CORE.PRESCRIBER`, with districts from `CORE.TERRITORY`. Every prescription matched a prescriber. I didn't use the draft Q2 realignment in the sandbox tables.
- **Last 13 weeks:** the 13 most recent complete Saturday–Friday weeks in the weekly prescription data, ending 3 Jul through 25 Sep 2026. I left out 26–30 Sep because that week isn't complete. The prior period is 28 Mar – 26 Jun 2026.
- **Abandonment reasons:** claims data (`RAW_3P.CLMLNS_ADJ`) with reject codes decoded using `REF.REJ_CODE`. This covers all territories combined only, because the claims don't identify the prescriber. Its counts are claim lines, so they don't match the patient-level totals.
- **Assumption:** I found no documented restrictions in the database, so I applied standard rules for third-party patient-level data (field teams see only territory-level totals). Please check with Compliance before sharing further.
