## Final answer

**Answer:** DC-EAST is already out of Lacrivia. If S-104's next RM-221 shipment ships two weeks late, the earliest the replacement supply reaches DC-EAST is about **10 Dec 2026**. When it arrives, the three **Tier A health systems get it first (Riverbend, Oak Hollow, Northfield)**, then the two Tier B systems (Clearview, Sunridge), then everyone else in proportion to their prescriptions.

**1. When DC-EAST runs out: it already has (about 26–27 Sep)**

| Pack | On hand (25 Sep snapshot) | Safety stock | Avg daily shipments, last 28 days | Days of supply | Stock-out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lacrivia 5 mL (FG-1001) | 46 | 471 | 36.7 | 1.3 | about 26 Sep |
| Lacrivia 10 mL (FG-1002) | 34 | 233 | 15.1 | 2.3 | about 27 Sep |

Both packs have been below safety stock since 11 Sep. The late shipment doesn't change the stock-out date; it makes the outage longer.

**2. When supply recovers: about 10 Dec 2026**
- The next open order is **PO005791**: 4,200 units of RM-221, promised to ship 7 Oct. Two weeks late means it ships **21 Oct**.
- It then takes **50 days** to become finished goods at the DC: 6 days transit to the premix supplier (S-201), 10 days to make, test and ship the premix to the plant, 15 days through the bulk and fill stages, then 19 days for packing, quality release (including the 14-day sterility test) and transit to the DC.
- So supply reaches DC-EAST around **10 Dec**, against about 26 Nov if the order shipped on time.
- That is an outage of roughly **75 days**, with about **3,900 units of demand not covered** (about 2,750 of the 5 mL and 1,130 of the 10 mL). The two-week delay alone adds about 725 units.
- **There's no backup supplier.** S-104 is the only qualified source of RM-221; the alternate, S-131, isn't qualified. The premix (CMP-310) also has a single source, S-201.
- **There's no company substitute.** Cyclosporine 0.05% (FG-1003) uses the same RM-221 and premix chain, so under the substitution rule there is no approved company substitute. Medical Affairs guides therapeutic alternatives.
